Marketing Campaign Summary Report: Campaign 201

Report Date: 2025-08-01

Campaign Date: 2024-08-18

Customer Segment: Environment Advocates

Campaign Topic: Sustainability Product Launch

Executive Summary

The Campaign 201 campaign targeted Environment Advocates with the objective to promote sustainability product launch. Out of an audience size of 162,771, emails were sent to 148,981 contacts, with a control group of 13,790. The campaign achieved an open rate of 20.04%, click rate of 8.21%, and a conversion rate of 2.89%, signifying strong engagement and effectiveness.

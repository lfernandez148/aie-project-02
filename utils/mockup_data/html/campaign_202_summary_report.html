Marketing Campaign Summary Report: Campaign 202

Report Date: 2025-08-01

Campaign Date: 2024-11-24

Customer Segment: Fitness Enthusiasts 18-40

Campaign Topic: Fitness Gear Promo

Executive Summary

The Campaign 202 campaign targeted Fitness Enthusiasts 18-40 with the objective to promote fitness gear promo. Out of an audience size of 186,787, emails were sent to 171,435 contacts, with a control group of 15,352. The campaign achieved an open rate of 17.64%, click rate of 3.58%, and a conversion rate of 1.62%, signifying strong engagement and effectiveness.
